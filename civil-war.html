March 6, 1820 — Missouri Compromise signed into law

Also known as the 'Compromise of 1820' was federal legislation set to balance the desires of both the Northern and Southern states in regards to the expansion of slavery. To keep the balance of power it admitted Missouri as a slave state and Maine as a free state and declared a policy of prohibiting slavery in the remaining Louisiana Purchase lands north of the 36°30′ parallel.

May 30, 1854 — Kansas Nebraska Act signed into law

This federal legislation created the territories of Kansas and Nebraska and stipulated that the settlers in these territories would determine the legality of slavery through "popular sovereignty". This effectively repealed the Missouri Compromise of 1820.
